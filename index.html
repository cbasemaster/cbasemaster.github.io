About

I work on machine learning methods for recognizing patterns in complex data, with a focus on temporal signals, deep neural networks, and explainable AI. My public research code explores COVID-19 growth prediction, environmental factors such as UV and pollution, mobility signals, and attribution methods for sequence models.
